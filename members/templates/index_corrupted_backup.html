AVIN SHUKRI PHARMACEUTICALS PRIVATE LIMITED

"ENDURING GOODNESS IN EVERY DOSE"

Avin Shukri Pharmaceuticals Private Limited is a rapidly growing pharmaceutical company, specializing in the marketing and distribution of high-quality affordable medicines, cosmetics and surgical products.

Our comprehensive product portfolio includes Injections, tablets, capsules, ointments, creams, dry syrups, and oral suspensions - serving both generic and branded markets with a strong presence in India and strategic international markets.

We are committed to addressing global healthcare needs across two continents backed by a focus on quality, regulatory compliance, and customer trust.

Avin shukri pharmaceuticals Pvt.Ltd, is highly committed to delivering high-quality pharmaceutical solutions with uncompromising standards guided by our philosophy of "Imperishable Goodness". We continuously strive to expand our reach and bring reliable healthcare solution to the universal markets.
