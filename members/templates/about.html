About Avin Shukri Pharmaceuticals

Avin Shukri Pharmaceuticals Private Limited is an emerging pharmaceutical marketing and export company headquartered in India. Founded in 2023, the company is focused on delivering high-quality pharmaceutical products through strong partnerships with certified manufacturers.

Our Comprehensive Product Portfolio:

  • Injections
  • Tablets
  • Capsules
  • Ointments
  • Creams
  • Dry Syrups
  • Oral Suspensions

The company currently manages a growing portfolio of pharmaceutical brands across multiple therapeutic segments and is steadily expanding its presence in international markets. With operations already established in the Republic of Chad and export activities expanding into several global regions.

We are committed to addressing global healthcare needs across two continents backed by a focus on quality, regulatory compliance, and customer trust, guided by our philosophy of "Imperishable Goodness".
